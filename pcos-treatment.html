PCOD / PCOS Care

PCOD / PCOS Management

PCOD and PCOS are common hormonal conditions that can affect your periods, skin, weight and fertility. At Dhanvantari Clinic, care starts with listening to your symptoms and history, followed by a treatment plan personalised to you.

Ask on WhatsApp

Who May Benefit / When to Consult

  • Irregular, heavy or absent periods
  • Symptoms you suspect may be linked to PCOD/PCOS, such as acne or unwanted hair growth
  • Difficulty conceiving where PCOD/PCOS may be a factor
  • Anyone recently diagnosed and looking for ongoing, personalised care

What This Involves

  • A detailed consultation about your cycle, symptoms and medical history
  • Guidance on lifestyle and dietary habits that can support hormonal balance
  • A personalised treatment plan, reviewed and adjusted as needed
  • Relevant investigations recommended and explained where appropriate

What to Expect

  • Your first visit will focus on understanding your symptoms and history in detail
  • Dr. Patil may recommend specific tests based on your individual case
  • You'll leave with a clear plan and the chance to ask every question you have
  • Follow-up visits let you and Dr. Patil track how things are progressing
