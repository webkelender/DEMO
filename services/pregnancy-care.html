Pregnancy Care

Pregnancy / Antenatal Care

From your first antenatal visit through to delivery, pregnancy care at Dhanvantari Clinic is built around regular checkups and an open relationship with your doctor — plus access to our Garbha Sanskar prenatal wellness sessions if you'd like to join.

Ask on WhatsApp

Who May Benefit / When to Consult

  • Women who are currently pregnant and need antenatal care
  • Women planning a pregnancy who want to prepare in advance
  • Anyone wanting a personalised antenatal checkup schedule
  • Expecting mothers interested in Garbha Sanskar sessions

What This Involves

  • Regular antenatal checkups (ANC) through your pregnancy
  • A personalised visit schedule based on your individual pregnancy
  • Access to periodic Garbha Sanskar prenatal wellness sessions — ask us for the next schedule
  • Open discussion of any concerns at every visit

What to Expect

  • Your first visit confirms your due date and sets up your checkup schedule
  • Your visit schedule is personalised by Dr. Patil and explained clearly to you
  • Bring your antenatal card to every visit once it's issued
  • You can raise questions or concerns at any checkup, big or small

Frequently Asked Questions

Your visit schedule is personalised by Dr. Patil based on your pregnancy and any risk factors, and she'll walk you through it at your first antenatal visit.
