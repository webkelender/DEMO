Our Story

Twenty years of laying the ground for good design.

TileCraft started in 2005 as a single stall in Bengaluru's tile market, sourcing overruns from local kilns. Today we run a 6,000 sq.ft. showroom stocked with ceramic, vitrified, marble-finish, wooden-finish and outdoor tiles from mills across India and Italy.

Every batch is checked by hand for shade consistency and edge finish before it reaches our floor — because a tile that looks right in the showroom should look right in your home too.
