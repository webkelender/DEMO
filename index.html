Our Story

Chairs built for the way you actually work.

SUNIL ASWARE is a seating showroom stocking ergonomic, executive, gaming, study and visitor chairs for offices, startups and homes.

Every chair is checked for build quality, gas-lift safety and stitching before it reaches our floor — because a chair that feels right in the showroom should feel right at your desk, every single day.
